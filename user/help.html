How does EcoTrack work?
EcoTrack connects you with local environmental initiatives. You complete tasks, verify them, and earn points which can be redeemed for rewards.
How are my points calculated?
Points are granted manually upon the review of your submitted tasks by the administration team. We ensure high-quality environmental contributions always get rewarded properly.
Why verify my account?
Account Verification ensures that the community is safe and transparent. Valid IDs and live facial matching protect us against bots and abuse.
Can I delete my account?
Yes. You can contact support directly to place a data removal request. Your information will be wiped securely according to our privacy protocols.
