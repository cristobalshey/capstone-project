ECOTRACK

Effective Date: November 26, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Ecotrack ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use our gamified recycling mobile web application ("Ecotrack" or the "App"). By using Ecotrack, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Name & Email Address
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Government-issued ID (photo or scan)
  • Selfie or profile photo
  • Photos of recyclable items for missions
  • Quiz answers
  • Location (for mapping user address)
  • Contact information for claiming rewards

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information (browser, OS, device type)
  • IP address
  • Usage behavior (missions completed, points earned, time spent)
  • Cookies for session management and analytics
  • Note: No sensitive biometric data is collected.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Verify your identity and prevent duplicate accounts
  • Validate recycling submissions
  • Award points and rewards fairly
  • Display leaderboards
  • Improve missions, quizzes, and game difficulty
  • Secure the app and prevent fraud
  • Communicate updates, reward notifications, and announcements

We do NOT sell your information, share your ID with advertisers, or share verification data publicly.

3. Why We Collect ID & Proof

  • Confirm users are real individuals
  • Prevent multiple accounts
  • Reduce fraud in reward redemption
  • Validate recycling activity
  • Improve credibility and security of the system

4. Storage & Protection

  • Encrypted database storage
  • Secure HTTPS connections
  • Limited admin access
  • Regular security audits
  • Secure deletion of old images

ID images and recycling proof are stored only as long as necessary for verification and auditing.

5. Data Sharing

We may share user information only with:

  • Authorized Ecotrack administrators
  • Third-party service providers (cloud hosting, storage, analytics)
  • Law enforcement when required by law

We do NOT sell or share data with marketing companies.

6. Data Retention

  • ID images: kept only until verification is complete
  • Recycling proof: stored temporarily for audit and fraud prevention
  • Account data: kept while your account is active
  • Deleted accounts: anonymized after deletion

7. User Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Request corrections
  • Request account or image deletion
  • Withdraw consent (may disable verification and reward features)
  • File a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority

8. Children’s Privacy

Ecotrack does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If a child has used the app, contact us for deletion.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced in the App.
