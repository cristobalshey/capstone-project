COLLECTOR TERMS

Effective Date: November 26, 2025

This Terms & Privacy Policy explains how Ecotrack ("we", "us", or "our") manages your obligations and protects your personal data when you use the Ecotrack Collector Portal (Collector Portal) as an authorized waste collector ("Collector").

1. Collector Obligations

  • Accurately verify and log all recyclable waste collected from users.
  • Use the Ecotrack Collector Portal exclusively for authorized collection activities.
  • Maintain a professional demeanor when interacting with Ecotrack users.
  • Do not manipulate, falsify, or submit fraudulent collection records.
  • Keep your collector credentials secure and do not share them.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Name & Email Address
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Government-issued ID (photo or scan for verification)
  • Selfie or profile photo

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information and IP address
  • Usage behavior (collections verified, time active, location during collections)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Verify your identity as an authorized Ecotrack Collector.
  • Audit collection histories to prevent fraud and ensure system integrity.
  • Communicate updates, collection route notifications, and announcements.

We do NOT sell your information, share your ID with advertisers, or share verification data publicly.

4. Why We Collect ID & Proof

  • Confirm you are a real, authorized individual representing Ecotrack.
  • Improve credibility and security of the waste collection system.

5. Storage & Protection

  • Encrypted database storage
  • Secure HTTPS connections
  • Limited admin access

6. Termination of Collector Privileges

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate your collector account if you are found violating these terms, falsifying records, or engaging in fraudulent activity on the platform.
