Verification

App verification serves as a digital "ID check" to ensure that the person using the service is who they say they are. It’s a layer of security designed to protect your data and the platform's integrity.

1. Security & Fraud Prevention

Apps use verification to stop bots and scammers from creating fake accounts.

Trust Anchor

Ensures hackers can't access your account without secondary verification (like a 2FA code).

Identity Proofing

Uses government ID or face scans to prevent identity theft and account takeovers.

2. Regulatory Compliance

KYC (Know Your Customer)

Legal requirement to prevent money laundering and illegal financing.

Age Gating

Ensures users meet minimum age requirements, protecting minors from inappropriate content.

3. Secure Reward Redemptions

Safe Payouts

Verification ensures that when you redeem your points, the rewards are sent directly to you and not to a fraudulent account.

Claim Protection

Prevents bad actors from using your identity to claim redemptions or cash-outs that you have worked hard to earn.
